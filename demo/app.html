Outgoing email (send quotes)

Quotes send from noreply@makerq.io. Customers see a note that the inbox isn't monitored and to reply to you, so set your notification email on the Account tab — replies go there. Uncheck the box above to use your own mailbox instead.

Send from your own mailbox (SMTP). Use an app password where supported, not your main password.

⚠ If your mailbox has 2-factor authentication (2FA) on, SMTP may not work — many providers (including GoDaddy) block password logins with 2FA and don’t offer app passwords for SMTP. Use MakerQ hosted email, or turn off 2FA on that mailbox. Also note: you can’t log in with an alias — use the real mailbox as the Username and put the alias in From address.

Incoming email (optional)

Pull customer emails from an inbox and turn them into quote requests. Skip this if you take requests through your intake form. Use an app password, not your main password.

Human check (CAPTCHA)

Stops bots submitting your forms. Uses Cloudflare Turnstile: free, no limits, and usually invisible to real customers.

How to get your two keys from Cloudflare
  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign in. A free account is enough, and it does not ask for a card.
  2. In the left menu pick Turnstile, then Add widget.
  3. Name it something you will recognise, for example MakerQ forms.
  4. Under Hostnames, add every domain your form sits on. Add the plain domain and the www one if you use both. A domain you leave out will fail the check.
  5. Leave the mode on Managed, then press Create.
  6. Copy the Site Key and Secret Key it shows you, and paste them below.

After saving, copy your embed code again: the check is built into the code when you copy it, so a form already pasted on your website will not have it yet.

Sticker Calculator
For printable sticker work on a Cricut, Silhouette or Brother cutter. A sticker job is priced by the sheet: print a sheet, laminate it, cut it, and that sheet's cost divides across however many stickers fit on it.
Stock: what a sheet costs and how many fit
Enter what a pack costs and how many sheets are in it — a $9.98 pack of 25 letter sheets is 40¢ a sheet, and MakerQ works that out. Add your own stock too: a 12 × 12 mat, 4 × 6 labels, or the same size in a different paper. Set how many of your sticker fit a letter sheet and press Fill sizes to estimate the rest by area — then correct them.
