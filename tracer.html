Outline Tracer

Turn a photo of your parts into a real-size outline

Included with Starter and Pro.

Making a drawer insert, a Gridfinity pocket, a tool tray or a foam cut-out means measuring awkward shapes. Instead, lay the parts on a sheet of paper, take a photo from straight above, and MakerQ traces their outlines at true size.

The paper is the ruler. MakerQ knows how big a Letter or A4 sheet is, so it corrects the angle of your photo and gets the scale right.

What you get

  • SVG and DXF in real millimetres, ready for Fusion, Onshape, Inkscape or your slicer
  • Drag the four corner markers for a tighter result: auto-detected corners land within about a millimetre, placed by hand around a tenth
  • Add clearance so the part drops into the pocket instead of jamming
  • Smooth the edges, ignore specks of dust, bridge a shiny edge that broke the outline, or keep a real hole like a handle opening
  • Works from a flatbed scan too, which skips the angle correction entirely

Tips for a good trace

  • Shoot from straight above, with the whole sheet in frame
  • Use plain white paper on a darker surface so the corners stand out
  • Check one object of known size before you print a whole insert
