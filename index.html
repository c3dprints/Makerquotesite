Collect, quote, produce, deliver

Every job follows the same four steps, and MakerQ handles each one.

1. Collect

Requests in one queue

A form you build in MakerQ and put on your website or Shopify store.

  • STL and 3MF files attached
  • Import requests from your inbox
  • Spam and bots kept off your board
2. Quote

A price from your costs

Set your filament, power, labour and markup once. Every job is priced the same way.

  • Warns you below your margin floor
  • Quote emails in your own words
  • Payment links in the email
3. Produce

Every job on a board

Drag jobs from New to Printing to Completed, with a production queue sorted by due date.

  • Folders and subfolders
  • Printer and stock tracking
  • Project numbers, like MQ-0001
4. Deliver

Customers kept in the loop

A private status page per order, then an invoice when it is done.

  • Approvals and design proofs
  • Invoices to print or email
  • Full history per customer

Your whole shop on one screen

Open quotes, jobs in progress, revenue and margin, without opening a spreadsheet.

Questions

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The live demo runs in your browser with sample data, no install and no sign-up. The Free plan is the real app with up to 5 active projects, and it needs no account and no card.

Does it run on Mac and Windows?

Yes, on Windows 10 and 11 and on macOS 12 or later. With MakerQ Cloud on Pro you can also use it in any web browser.

Where is my data kept?

On the desktop app, on your own computer. In MakerQ Cloud, on our servers, with every shop kept separate. Read exactly what leaves your computer and when.

Can I switch from the desktop app to Cloud later?

Yes. In the desktop app, open Settings and choose MakerQ Cloud. It copies your projects, customers, files and settings, and the copy on your computer stays as it was.

Do I need an account?

Not for the desktop app. You only create an account if you use MakerQ Cloud, and paid plans are bought inside the app.
