Your data

How MakerQ handles your data

Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version. You can run MakerQ two ways:

  1. As a desktop app on Windows or macOS. Your quotes, customers and files live in a database on your own computer. No account is needed to start, and the Free plan does not use any MakerQ server. The only thing it checks online is GitHub, for app updates.
  2. As MakerQ Cloud at app.makerq.io, the same app in your web browser, run on servers we operate, with every shop's data kept separate from every other shop's.

Both are the same product with the same features. You choose, and you can move from the desktop app to Cloud whenever you like. The copy on your computer stays exactly as it was.

Where your data lives on the desktop app

On your computer. MakerQ keeps your projects, customers, pricing settings and quote history in a database file in the MakerQ data folder, with uploaded 3D files and photos next to it. You can back the folder up like any other files, and paid plans add a settings backup in the app.

  • MakerQ works offline. Your shop does not stop if your internet does.
  • We cannot see your customers, prices or files. We do not have them.
  • If you downgrade or cancel, features above your plan switch off, but your data stays put and the app keeps opening.
  • The Free plan needs no account, no card and no sign-in.

How MakerQ Cloud keeps shops separate

MakerQ Cloud runs the same app on Render, in the United States (Oregon). Many shops share the same servers, so the important question is how one shop is kept from seeing another.

  • The database enforces it. Every row of data is tagged with the shop it belongs to, and PostgreSQL row-level security only lets a signed-in shop read or write its own rows. Because the database enforces this, not just the app, an app bug cannot on its own expose one shop's data to another.
  • Files are private. Photos and 3D files are kept in a private storage bucket. They are never public: the app only hands out a link to a file after checking it belongs to your shop, and every link expires after an hour.
  • Accounts. Every account confirms its email address with a code before it works. Your email and password only ever open your own shop, and people you invite only join your shop. Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, never as the password itself, and repeated failed sign-in attempts are temporarily blocked.
  • Encryption in transit. Everything between your browser and app.makerq.io is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Backups. The Cloud database is backed up continuously, and can be restored to any point in at least the past three days.
  • Who can get in. Only the MakerQ team, which today is our founder, has access to the production systems.

What Cloud does not have yet: there is no independent security audit or SOC 2 report. MakerQ is built and run by a small team, and we would rather tell you that than suggest otherwise.

Moving between the desktop app and Cloud

In the desktop app, open Settings and choose MakerQ Cloud. It creates your Cloud shop, copies your projects, customers, uploaded files and settings, and gives you the link to open it. The copy on your computer is left exactly as it was.

Getting your data out of Cloud: you can export your customer list as a CSV file and back up your settings from the app. A one-click export of a whole Cloud shop is not built yet. If you need a full copy, or want your Cloud shop closed and its data deleted, contact us.

Your customers' data

Your customers trust you with their names, emails, addresses and design files. MakerQ is built so that trust does not have to extend to us.

  • On the desktop app, customer data stays on your computer unless you turn on one of the hosted features above.
  • A customer's status page shows only their own order. It needs no account, and it never shows other orders or other customers.
  • We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use it for advertising. MakerQ makes its money from plan subscriptions.

Plans

  • Free, $0: up to 5 active projects, list and Kanban views, the roll calculator with inventory tracking, and the Sticker Calculator. No account.
  • Starter, $4 a month or $48 a year: unlimited projects, your own material and labour costs, Quick Quote, the Outline Tracer, sending quotes, payment links, invoices, CSV import and export, shop links and settings backup.
  • Pro, $8.99 a month or $89.99 a year: everything in Starter, plus AI help, customer status and approval pages, website and Shopify forms, hosted email, network sharing and MakerQ Cloud.

Lifetime licenses are no longer sold. Anyone who already has one keeps every feature they had, including MakerQ Cloud at no extra cost. There are no per-quote fees, and nothing extra for the number of customers or files.

Who builds MakerQ

MakerQ is made by C3D Prints, a 3D print shop. It started as the tool we built to run our own quotes and order queue, and we still run our own orders on it every day.
